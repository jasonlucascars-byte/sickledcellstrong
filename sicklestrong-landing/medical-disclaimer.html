Medical Disclaimer

Draft · last updated 24 July 2026

SickleStrong is an organizing tool, not medical care

SickleStrong helps families organize sickle cell care information and provides care-plan reminders. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any illness, and it is not a substitute for professional medical judgment. Information in the app — including reminders, summaries, and any thresholds — is for organization and general awareness only.

Always rely on your care team

SickleStrong does not replace your hematologist, medical team, primary care provider, emergency department, or emergency services. Always follow the guidance of your own care team and your child's care plan. Never disregard professional medical advice, or delay seeking it, because of something you saw or logged in SickleStrong.

In an emergency

If you or your child is experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not use the app to manage an emergency.

About the fever reminder

SickleStrong can show a reminder when a logged temperature reaches 101.3°F (38.5°C) or higher, because fever can be an emergency for people with sickle cell disease. This reminder is a prompt to follow your care team's instructions and seek urgent evaluation — it is not a diagnosis and not a guarantee that any particular reading is or is not serious. Follow your care team's plan, which may differ.

No provider–patient relationship

Using SickleStrong does not create a doctor–patient or other professional–client relationship between you and SickleStrong. We are not your healthcare provider.

Accuracy and your responsibility

The information shown reflects what you and approved caregivers enter. Please verify important details with your care team. You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you record.

Questions
