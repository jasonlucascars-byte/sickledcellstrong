Why SickleStrong

Less searching. Better recall. More prepared conversations.

Built from lived experience

I needed one place for two sons at two different stages of care.

I'm Jason Lucas, the father of two sons living with sickle cell disease. One was learning to manage more of his own health while the other still needed daily parent-led care. Their medications, symptoms, appointments, and needs were different — but the responsibility to remember everything was the same.

SickleStrong began as the tool I wished our family already had: one organized place for the daily details, a clearer record for doctor visits, and support for families caring for more than one person.
